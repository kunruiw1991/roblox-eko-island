# eko Beta Test

Cozy pastel cel-shaded 3D hex-grid island builder and 3rd-person R6 explorer built with Three.js r170.

Live play: https://kunruiw1991.github.io/roblox-eko-island/

## Features

- **Hexagonal Terraforming Sandbox** — 331 interactive hex columns (radius 10) with sedimentary cream/tan cliff tiers (levels 1–5), bevelled lime-green grass caps, sparkling carved turquoise freshwater ponds, pulsing coastal foam collars, drifting 3D cartoon clouds, and gentle breeze wind lines.
- **Modular Props & Cozy Cottages** — Plant Oak trees, Pine trees, Flowering Bushes, Sedimentary Rocks, and rustic half-timber Cottages with bouncy spring-pop animations and pitch-scaled WebAudio marimba notes.
- **Contextual Radial Menu** — Tap any placed cottage, tree, bush, or rock to pop a 4-way tactile ring: **Rotate** (`15°` or `60°` snap), **Duplicate** (copies to nearest open neighbor hex), **Recolor** (5 cozy pastel palettes: Terracotta, Sage, Buttercup, Sky Blue, Berry Pink), and **Delete**.
- **8 One-Tap Island Presets** (`Presets 1–8`):
  1. Starter Meadow Cove
  2. Twin Cliff Lighthouse
  3. Heart Pond Oasis
  4. Pine Mountain Ridge
  5. Pastel Harbor Town
  6. Spiral Terrace Garden
  7. Cozy Archipelago
  8. Blank Ocean Canvas
- **3rd-Person R6 Dummy Walk Mode** — Switch seamlessly between floating builder view and walking around your island as a smooth light-gray Roblox R6 dummy avatar with auto-step hill climbing, cliff jumping, pond splashes, and a joyful wave/spin emote.
- **Auto-Save & Undo** — Local browser persistence (`roblox_eko_island_save`) plus 40-step Undo history.

## Controls

### iPad / Touch
- **Build Mode**: Single tap to sculpt or decorate; tap an existing prop for the Radial Menu; 1-finger drag to orbit; 2-finger pinch to zoom and gently pan.
- **Walk Mode**: Left thumbstick to walk (camera-relative, zero auto-orbit), right-side **JUMP** and **WAVE** buttons (multi-touch ready), 1-finger drag on canvas to look around, tap **BUILD MODE** to return anytime.

### Desktop / Keyboard
- **Build Mode**: Left-click to place/sculpt or open Radial Menu; drag to orbit; scroll wheel to zoom; `1`–`9` (or `V` Oak, `C` Pine) to switch tools; `Z` Undo; `R` Rotate selected; `Delete` Delete selected.
- **Walk Mode**: `WASD` / Arrow keys to move, `Space` Jump, `E` Wave emote, drag to orbit camera.
- **Global**: `Tab` toggle Walk/Build Mode, `Esc` close radial/modals or open Settings.
